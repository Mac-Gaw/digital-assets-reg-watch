# Site Identity

Digital Assets Regulatory Watch is an informational regulatory intelligence workspace.

## Intended categorisation

Suitable public categorisation labels may include:

- Business
- Financial services information
- Reference
- Regulatory research
- Professional services research
- News and media / business news, where no regulatory/research category exists

## Not intended categorisation

The site is not designed as:

- cryptocurrency trading,
- investment advice,
- gambling,
- wallet services,
- exchange services,
- portfolio tracking,
- price data or market speculation,
- token promotion,
- DeFi yield or staking service.

## Functional boundaries

MVP v1.0 does not include:

- user accounts,
- wallet connection,
- trading execution,
- price feeds,
- exchange APIs,
- advertising scripts,
- payment functionality,
- comment systems,
- analytics trackers.

The site provides public-source links, metadata, summaries, categorisation and regulatory-monitoring workflow support.
