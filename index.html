Regulatory access matrix

Institutional permissions & perimeter navigator

Explore high-level regulatory routes by jurisdiction, entity type, activity and asset class. Initial coverage includes selected United Kingdom, European Union and United States use cases.

01

Classification comes first

A tokenised financial instrument may remain subject to securities legislation rather than MiCA or the UK qualifying-cryptoasset perimeter.

02

Activity matters more than ownership

Holding an asset for an institution's own account is different from custody, execution, arranging, issuance or operating market infrastructure for clients.

High-level information only. This matrix is a research aid based on public primary sources. It is not legal advice, a definitive perimeter assessment or confirmation that a particular institution may undertake an activity. Entity-specific permissions, local implementation, transitional arrangements, mandate restrictions and prudential rules must be checked separately.

About

Workspace overview

Purpose, intended users and operating boundaries for the workspace.

What this workspace is

Regulatory intelligence for institutional digital assets use cases

Digital Assets Regulatory Watch is a source-led monitoring workspace covering public regulatory developments, consultations, historical milestones and selected institutional permissions scenarios across digital assets, tokenisation, stablecoins and financial market infrastructure.

Primary users

Designed for regulated institutions and control functions

Banks Custodians Asset managers Fund platforms Legal & compliance Market infrastructure teams

Included modules

Built around core research workflows

  • Curated regulatory feed with current developments and selected historical milestones
  • Consultation tracker for work in progress and implementation timing
  • Regulatory Access Matrix for selected UK, EU and US scenarios
  • Coverage & methodology layer describing source hierarchy and classification logic

Boundaries

Research support, not legal advice or execution tooling

Disclaimer: This workspace does not provide investment advice, trading signals, wallet services, cryptoasset pricing, portfolio tracking or execution functionality. It is a public-source research resource and does not replace legal, regulatory or prudential analysis.
